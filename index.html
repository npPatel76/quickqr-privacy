Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

1. Information Collection & Storage

quickQR is built with an offline-first architecture to prioritize user privacy and data security.

  • Offline Local Storage: All scanned QR codes, product barcodes, and generated QR codes are stored locally on your device using an offline SQLite database. We do NOT collect or store your scan history on any remote server.
  • Camera Access: Camera permission is used solely for real-time QR code and barcode scanning. Camera video feeds are processed entirely on-device and are never recorded or transmitted.
  • Photo Gallery Access: Storage access is used only when you choose to scan a QR image from your gallery or export a generated high-resolution QR code image.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To keep quickQR free, we integrate Google AdMob for serving non-intrusive advertisements. AdMob may process device identifiers and performance metrics to deliver relevant ads.

  • Learn more about Google Privacy Policy
  • Learn more about Google Advertising Technologies

3. Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

We comply with international data protection regulations including European GDPR and California CCPA:

  • GDPR (European Union): You can delete all your stored history data at any time directly in the app settings or by clearing the app data.
  • CCPA (California): We do NOT sell or share personal user data with third parties.

4. Children’s Privacy

quickQR does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
