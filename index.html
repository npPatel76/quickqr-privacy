# Privacy Policy for quickQR **Effective Date**: September 27, 2026 **quickQR** ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information. --- ### 1. Information Collection & Storage - **Offline Local Storage**: All scanned QR codes, product barcodes, and custom generated QR codes are stored locally on your device using an offline database (SQLite). We do NOT transmit, collect, or store your scan history on any external server. - **Camera Access**: Camera permission is required solely for real-time scanning of QR codes and product barcodes. Camera data is processed entirely on-device and is never recorded or uploaded. - **Photo Gallery Access**: Gallery access is required only when you choose to scan a QR code from an image or export a high-resolution QR code image to your device storage. --- ### 2. Third-Party Services & Advertisements **quickQR** uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage metrics to deliver relevant ad experiences. - **Google AdMob Privacy Policy**: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) - **Google Ad Technologies**: [https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) --- ### 3. International Privacy Compliance (GDPR / CCPA) - **European Union (GDPR)**: Users have the right to request deletion of any locally stored data simply by clearing the history inside the app settings or uninstalling the app. - **California (CCPA)**: We do not sell user personal information to third parties. --- ### 4. Children’s Privacy **quickQR** does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. --- ### 5.
